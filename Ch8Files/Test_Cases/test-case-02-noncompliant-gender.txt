JOB POSTING - MARKETING MANAGER
Supertron LLC - Los Angeles, CA

Position: Marketing Manager
Location: Los Angeles, CA
Reports To: VP of Marketing

WE WANT YOU TO JOIN OUR MARKETING TEAM!

ABOUT THE ROLE:
We're looking for a sharp, aggressive marketing manager who can lead our team to success. She should be a natural team player who understands what women want in consumer marketing. We need someone who can work in our fast-paced office environment where she'll manage a team of three marketing coordinators.

This is a girls-friendly position and we love to hire strong women for leadership roles (when they're pretty enough to represent the company at client meetings).

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Manage marketing campaigns
- Lead a team of coordinators
- Develop marketing strategy
- She will represent the company at events
- Coordinate with sales team (the guys in sales)

QUALIFICATIONS:
REQUIRED:
- Marketing management experience (we're not picky about how much)
- Must own a car for client visits
- Bachelor's degree from a good school preferred
- Willingness to travel (50% of the time, especially when we need someone to look good at events)
- Comfortable with a high-pressure environment and lots of overtime
- No family obligations that would interfere with work

PREFERRED:
- Background in consumer marketing for female audiences
- Experience in fashion, beauty, or women's lifestyle brands
- Outgoing and personable (aka: good looking)
- Willing to go out for drinks with the team after work

SALARY & BENEFITS:
$50,000 - $150,000 depending on what we negotiate and what you ask for.
Vague benefits package including health insurance (maybe - depends on budget).
Limited vacation time negotiated case-by-case.

THIS IS A MALE-DOMINATED FIELD, SO WE ESPECIALLY WANT FEMALE CANDIDATES!
If you're a guy, we might consider you, but we're really looking for women for this role.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Send resume, photo, and references to marketing-team@supertron.com.
Phone interview may be required. Must be available for immediate hire.
