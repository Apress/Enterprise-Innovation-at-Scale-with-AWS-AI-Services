JOB POSTING - EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT
Supertron LLC - San Francisco, CA

Position: Executive Assistant to the CEO
Location: San Francisco, CA - ON-SITE ONLY
Reports To: CEO

WANTED: THE PERFECT EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT

We're seeking the right "office mother figure" to manage our C-level team's schedules, travel, and personal needs. This is an insane role with crazy deadlines and requires someone who can handle constant pressure.

ROLE SUMMARY:
You'll be responsible for managing the CEO's calendar, scheduling meetings, coordinating travel, and handling administrative tasks. This position requires someone who can multitask without going crazy and maintain a positive attitude even when things are lame and the work is dumb.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Manage CEO and executive calendars
- Coordinate executive travel (20% of time)
- Manage expense reports
- Arrange meetings and events
- Support up to 5 executives simultaneously
- Work independently with minimal guidance

REQUIREMENTS:
REQUIRED (NO EXCEPTIONS):
- At least 20+ years of executive assistant experience (no alternatives)
- Perfect physical health - no disabilities or health issues
- Must be able to work 50+ hours per week without complaining
- Willing to sacrifice personal life for work (be prepared to work nights/weekends as needed)
- Master's degree in business administration or equivalent (no substitute)
- Available to start immediately (no notice period)

PREFERRED:
- Unpaid internship background showing willingness to work for free
- Never taken a sick day
- No family responsibilities or dependents
- Willing to relocate permanently
- Perfect credit score (we verify this as part of background check)
- Former military background

PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Must be able to walk around office all day without needing mobility assistance
- Cannot have any physical limitations
- Able to stand for 8+ hours at a time
- No deaf or hard of hearing candidates (we use verbal communication only, no accommodations)
- No mental health issues or history of mental illness
- Must be "normal" and mentally stable

SCHEDULE:
- Monday-Friday, 8am-6pm minimum
- Weekend work required during busy periods
- No flexibility
- Must be available for last-minute schedule changes
- Overtime not compensated

SALARY:
$45,000 - $55,000 per year depending on how much experience you have. No negotiation.
Benefits TBD based on budget.

ACCESSIBILITY:
We have an accessible office facility. Wheelchair access available if needed, but we've never had someone in a wheelchair work here so this is a new situation for us.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Email resume and full background check release to hr@supertron.com
Phone interviews only (sorry, no video options available).
Must be available for interview within 24 hours of contact.
