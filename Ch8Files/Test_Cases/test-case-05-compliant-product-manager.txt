JOB POSTING - PRODUCT MANAGER
Supertron LLC - Remote (US-based)

Position: Product Manager
Location: Remote (US-based, across time zones welcome)
Department: Product Management
Reports To: VP of Product
Job Type: Full-Time

ABOUT THE ROLE:
Supertron LLC is seeking a Product Manager to lead the strategy and execution of one of our core product lines. In this role, you'll work cross-functionally with engineering, design, sales, and customer success to define product vision, prioritize roadmap, and drive successful launches. You'll be responsible for understanding customer needs, translating them into product requirements, and measuring success.

This is a high-impact role where you'll have significant influence on the direction of our product and the company's growth.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Define product vision and strategy (25% of time)
- Gather and synthesize customer and market feedback (20% of time)
- Work with engineering and design to deliver features (30% of time)
- Analyze product metrics and iterate based on data (15% of time)
- Collaborate with go-to-market teams on launches (10% of time)

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- 5+ years of product management experience (or equivalent demonstrated expertise in defining and shipping products)
- Track record of driving product strategy and execution
- Strong analytical skills with ability to use data to inform decisions
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Experience working in cross-functional environments with engineering and design
- Bachelor's degree in any field, OR equivalent professional experience

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- Experience in B2B SaaS products
- Background in analytics, business intelligence, or data platforms
- Experience with Agile/Scrum methodologies
- Familiarity with product management tools (JIRA, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.)
- Previous startup or high-growth company experience

DESIRED ATTRIBUTES:
- You're customer-obsessed and driven by understanding user needs
- You think strategically but can dive deep into details when needed
- You're comfortable with ambiguity and can make decisions with incomplete information
- You're collaborative and build strong relationships across teams
- You're resilient and handle setbacks as learning opportunities
- You're passionate about continuous improvement and iteration

ABOUT OUR TEAM:
Our product team is distributed across the US with team members from diverse backgrounds and perspectives. We value inclusion, psychological safety, and collaborative problem-solving. You'll work with talented engineers, designers, and leaders who are committed to shipping great products.

WORK ARRANGEMENT:
This is a fully remote position. You can work from anywhere in the US with reliable internet. We have team meetings with flexible scheduling to accommodate different time zones. If you prefer occasional in-person collaboration, we have office space available in San Francisco and New York for optional use.

CAREER GROWTH:
We believe in developing our talent. You'll have a clear path to senior product leadership roles and opportunities to mentor junior product managers. We provide regular feedback, career development conversations, and investment in your growth.

SCHEDULE & FLEXIBILITY:

- Fully flexible schedule outside core hours
- Async-first communication to support distributed team
- We respect work-life balance and don't expect responses outside working hours

INTERVIEW PROCESS:
1. Initial conversation with recruiting (30 minutes, phone/video)
2. Take-home case study or writing exercise (2-3 hours, completed on your timeline)
3. Product strategy conversation with VP Product (60 minutes)
4. Team interviews with 2-3 cross-functional partners (30 minutes each)
5. Final conversation with VP Product (30 minutes)

We aim to complete the process within 2-3 weeks. If you need accommodations during the interview process (alternative formats, extra time, accessible meeting rooms, interpreters, etc.), please let us know and we'll work with you.
