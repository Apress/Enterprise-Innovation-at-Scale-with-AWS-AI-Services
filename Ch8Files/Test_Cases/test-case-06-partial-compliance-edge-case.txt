JOB POSTING - HR COORDINATOR
Supertron LLC - San Francisco, CA

Position: HR Coordinator
Location: San Francisco, CA (On-site)
Department: Human Resources
Reports To: HR Manager
Job Type: Full-Time

ABOUT THE ROLE:
Supertron LLC is seeking an HR Coordinator to support our HR team and assist with day-to-day HR operations. You'll help manage employee records, coordinate onboarding for new hires, process benefits, and support our HR team with various administrative and operational tasks.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Maintain employee records and HR database (35% of time)
- Coordinate onboarding process for new hires (25% of time)
- Process benefits enrollment and changes (20% of time)
- Schedule meetings and manage calendars for HR team (10% of time)
- Support special projects as needed (10% of time)

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- 2+ years HR experience or equivalent administrative experience in an HR function
- Strong organizational and attention to detail
- Proficiency with HR systems and tools (HRIS, ATS, etc.)
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to handle confidential information appropriately
- High school diploma or equivalent; some college coursework or professional certification preferred

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- SHRM-CP or other HR certification
- Experience with benefits administration
- Proficiency with Google Workspace or Microsoft Office
- Customer service experience in HR or similar field

ABOUT YOU:
- You're detail-oriented and organized
- You're a good communicator and enjoy helping others
- You're reliable and can be trusted with sensitive information
- You're flexible and adapt to changing priorities
- You have a positive attitude and work well in a team environment
- You understand the importance of confidentiality in HR

WORK LOCATION:
This is an on-site position in our San Francisco office. We expect you to be in the office 5 days per week during standard business hours (9am-5pm). Remote work is not available for this role due to the sensitive nature of HR data.

COMPENSATION:
Salary: $50,000 - $65,000 per year, based on experience

Benefits:
- Health insurance (medical, dental, vision)
- 401(k) with 3% employer match
- 15 days paid time off + 10 company holidays
- Wellness program
- Professional development support

WORK ENVIRONMENT:
This role involves working with employees at all levels and requires discretion and professionalism. You'll work closely with the HR team and be the first point of contact for HR-related employee questions. The position may involve occasional emotionally challenging conversations related to performance issues, terminations, or personal circumstances.
