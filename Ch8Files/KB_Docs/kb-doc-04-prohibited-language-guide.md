# Prohibited Language Reference Guide

## Quick Reference: Terms to Avoid & Compliant Alternatives

Use this guide as a quick reference when writing job postings. Each category lists problematic terms and better alternatives.

---

## CATEGORY 1: AGE-BIASED LANGUAGE

### Direct Age References

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Young" | Directly implies age; ADEA violation | "Motivated," "Enthusiastic" |
| "Recent graduate" | Limits to young applicants | "Entry-level candidates welcome" or "Early career opportunities available" |
| "Digital native" | Proxy for youth; generational | "Proficient with modern tools and technologies" |
| "Energetic" | Often paired with age to indicate youth | "Motivated," "Driven," "Passionate" |
| "Tech-savvy millennial" | Explicitly age/generation-coded | "Skilled with current technologies" or "Quick to learn new tools" |
| "Old school thinking not wanted" | Directly excludes older workers | Remove entirely or say "Innovative approaches valued" |
| "Startup energy" | Implies youth and unsustainable hours | "Fast-paced environment" or "Growth-oriented role" |

### Age Proxy Language

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "All-nighters required" | Often excludes older workers with family obligations | "Deadline-driven role" or "Occasional extended hours may be needed" |
| "Comfortable with constant change" | Youth proxy; implies chaos | "Adaptable to evolving business needs" |
| "Cutting-edge tech stack only" | Excludes experienced workers with legacy tech | "Strong foundation in current and emerging technologies" |
| "Stay current with trends" | Implies older workers fall behind | "Committed to continuous learning" |
| "Fast-paced startup culture" | Youth-coded; implies work-life balance issues | "Growth-focused environment" or "Dynamic role with variety" |
| "10+ years required" | Suspect as age proxy if not truly necessary | "Significant expertise in [specific domain]" |
| "Must keep up with us" | Ageist implication | "Collaborative team seeking high performers" |

## CATEGORY 2: GENDER-BIASED LANGUAGE

### Gendered Job Titles

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Salesmen" | Male-only | "Sales professionals" |
| "Fireman" | Male-only | "Firefighter" |
| "Hostess" | Female-only | "Host" or "Hospitality professional" |
| "Girl Friday" | Female stereotype | "Administrative professional" |
| "Chairman" | Male-default | "Chair" or "Chairperson" |
| "Cameraman" | Male-only | "Camera operator" |
| "Stewardess" | Female-only | "Flight attendant" |

### Gendered Pronouns

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "He/she will manage..." | Assumes binary gender; awkward | "The successful candidate will..." or use "they" |
| "His or her" | Unnecessarily gendered | "Their" (singular they is grammatically correct) |
| "Manpower" | Male-default term | "Staff," "Personnel," "Workforce" |
| "Mankind" | Male-default | "Humanity," "People," "Everyone" |

### Gender Stereotypes in Language

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Nurturing, caring, maternal" | Stereotypically female | "Empathetic," "Patient," "Supportive" |
| "Aggressive, driven, leader" | Stereotypically male (penalizes women for same behavior) | "Ambitious," "Results-oriented," "Takes initiative" |
| "Team player" (when describing women) | Often code for "doesn't push back"; expect more from women | Use consistently for all genders or be specific: "Collaborates effectively" |
| "Bossy" | Typically applied to women in leadership | "Assertive," "Decisive," "Takes charge" |
| "Ambitious" (when describing women) | Can be coded negatively; implies self-interest | Use consistently; note that "ambitious" is positive |
| "Attractive, pretty, good-looking" | Objectifying; unprofessional | Remove entirely; never mention physical appearance |
| "Mother/father of the office" | Gender-stereotyping | "Team connector," "Mentor," "Senior colleague" |
| "Sensitive, emotional" | Often applied to women negatively | "Emotionally intelligent," "Aware of team dynamics" |

## CATEGORY 3: ABLEIST LANGUAGE

### Terms Using Disability as Metaphor/Insult

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Crazy" (deadline, hours) | Stigmatizes mental illness | "Challenging," "Fast-paced," "Demanding" |
| "Insane" (as intensifier) | Stigmatizes mental illness | "Intense," "Demanding," "Rigorous" |
| "Lame" | Offensive to people with mobility disabilities | "Weak," "Poor," "Inadequate" |
| "Dumb" | Offensive to people with intellectual disabilities | "Uninformed," "Unclear," "Misguided" |
| "Blind spot" | Offensive to blind/low-vision people | "Overlooked," "Missed," "Unaware" |
| "Deaf to feedback" | Offensive to deaf/hard of hearing | "Unresponsive," "Ignores," "Dismissive" |
| "OCD" (as a joke) | Trivializes mental health condition | Don't use; be specific: "Detail-oriented," "Organized" |
| "Bipolar" (mood changes) | Stigmatizes mental health condition | "Moody," "Inconsistent," "Unpredictable" |
| "Psycho," "Psychotic" | Stigmatizing | Don't use; be specific about the actual problem |
| "Retarded" (as insult) | Deeply offensive | Use appropriate descriptor: "Slow," "Backward," "Delayed" |

### Assumptions About Disability

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Must be physically fit/able-bodied" | Excludes people with disabilities | List specific essential functions; mention accommodations |
| "Normal candidates only" | "Normal" implies disabled = abnormal | Use specific criteria: "Candidates with [skills/credentials]" |
| "Wheelchair access only if needed" | Implies disability is unusual exception | "Fully accessible facilities including wheelchair access" |
| "We're mentally healthy workplace" | Implies others aren't; problematic | "Supportive work environment," "Mental health resources available" |
| "No mental illness" | Can't screen for this; illegal | Remove entirely |
| "Vision/hearing required" | Exclude blind/deaf people if not truly essential | Specify: "Ability to [actual essential function] required; accommodations available" |

### Problematic Framing

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Despite their disability" | Inspiration porn framing | Don't mention disability at all; focus on qualifications |
| "Special needs" | Outdated, infantilizing | "Individuals with disabilities," "Accessibility needs" |
| "Suffers from" | Negative framing | "Person with [condition]" or just focus on ability to do job |
| "Confined to a wheelchair" | Negative framing | "Wheelchair user" |

## CATEGORY 4: RACE/ETHNICITY/NATIONAL ORIGIN

### Direct Discrimination

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "White candidates preferred" | Explicit discrimination | Don't mention race at all |
| "No immigrants" | National origin discrimination | Remove entirely |
| "Must be born in US" | National origin discrimination (unless legally required) | "Must be authorized to work in US" |
| "No accent" | Proxy for national origin | Remove entirely |
| "English-only" (when not truly needed) | Can discriminate against bilingual speakers | "Fluent English communication required" only if truly needed for role |
| "American citizens only" (unless legally mandated) | National origin discrimination | "Must have valid work authorization" |

### Coded Language

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Fit our culture" (when used with race-coded requirements) | Can be proxy for "people like us" | Be specific about actual cultural values needed |
| "We're looking for someone like us" | Often excludes minorities | Remove this framing entirely |
| Requiring "American" background/experience | Can be proxy for race or national origin | Specify actual experience needed: "5+ years in US enterprise software" |

## CATEGORY 5: RELIGION

### Direct Discrimination

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Christian values preferred" | Religious discrimination | Remove entirely unless it's a religious organization position |
| "Must pray with team" | Religious discrimination; job requirement? | Remove entirely |
| "Sabbath-observant need not apply" | Religious discrimination | Offer flexible scheduling for all religions |
| "Atheists welcome only" | Religious discrimination | Don't mention religion at all |
| "Judeo-Christian ethics" | Excludes other religions | Remove entirely |

## CATEGORY 6: SOCIOECONOMIC STATUS

### Education/Background Discrimination

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Ivy League only" | Excludes qualified candidates from other backgrounds | "Bachelor's degree in [field]" or "Equivalent professional experience" |
| "Top-tier university required" | Discriminatory; excludes state school graduates | "Bachelor's degree from accredited university" or "Equivalent experience" |
| "Unpaid internship" | Excludes people who can't afford to work for free | "Paid internship" or remove requirement |
| "Unpaid trial period" | Excludes low-income candidates | "Paid trial period" or remove requirement |
| "Must own car" (when driving not essential) | Excludes those without means | Only require if driving is essential: "Valid driver's license required for [specific reason]" |

### Implicit Financial Requirements

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Perfect credit score required" | Often unrelated to job performance; discriminates against poor | Only require if truly necessary (e.g., financial role handling client money): "Must pass background check" |
| "Background check including financial history" (non-financial role) | Unnecessary; may discriminate | "Criminal background check" only if job-related |
| "No bankruptcy history" | Discriminatory | Remove entirely unless truly job-related |

## CATEGORY 7: INDUSTRY JARGON & CODED LANGUAGE

### Youth-Coded Jargon

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Rockstar" developer | Youth-coded compliment; feels exclusionary | "Expert," "Senior specialist," "Highly skilled" |
| "Ninja" | Youth-coded; implies outsider status | "Expert," "Specialist" |
| "Guru" | Youth-coded; implies unrealistic expectations | "Expert," "Senior," "Specialist" |
| "Unicorn" | Youth-coded; implies impossible standards | "Exceptional candidate" or list specific skills needed |
| "10x engineer" | Youth-coded; unrealistic expectations | List specific skills and outcomes needed |

### Startup Culture Red Flags

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Move fast and break things" | Implies chaos, unsustainable pace, no accountability | "Agile," "Iterative," "Continuous improvement" |
| "Live and breathe our mission" | Demands personal identity merger with company | "Passionate about [specific mission element]" |
| "Work hard, play hard" | Implies excessive drinking, unsustainable hours | "Balanced work environment" or "Social opportunities" |
| "Hustle culture" | Glorifies overwork; excludes those with obligations | "Results-oriented," "Goal-focused" |
| "Disruption" | Vague startup speak; can mean chaos | Be specific: "Innovation," "New approaches," etc. |

### Vague Culture Language

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "We're a family" | Sets up boundary-crossing, emotional labor demands | "Collaborative environment," "Supportive team" |
| "Drinks with team are required" | Social coercion; excludes non-drinkers, parents, caregivers | "Optional team social events" |
| "You'll love it here" | Vague; presumptuous | Describe specific appealing aspects |
| "Flexible" (without details) | Meaningless; often false | "Remote," "4-day week," "flexible hours" (be specific) |
| "Unlimited PTO" | Often means "unclear expectations" and "pressure not to take time" | Specify: "20 days PTO" or "Flexible time off with manager approval" |

## CATEGORY 8: GENDERED STEREOTYPES IN COMPETENCIES

| Prohibited (When Applied Selectively) | Why | Better Practice |
|---|---|---|
| "Empathetic" for women; "Strategic" for men | Different standards for same role | Use consistently across all genders |
| "Team player" for women; "Leader" for men | Different standards for same role | Use consistently across all genders |
| "Detail-oriented" for women; "Big-picture thinker" for men | Different standards for same role | Use consistently across all genders |
| "Communicative" for women; "Decisive" for men | Different standards for same role | Use consistently across all genders |

## CATEGORY 9: APPEARANCE/PHYSICALITY

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Attractive appearance preferred" | Discriminatory and unprofessional | Remove entirely |
| "Good-looking team members" | Discriminatory; objectifying | Remove entirely |
| "Tall preferred" (non-essential) | Discriminatory | Only include if truly essential with job justification |
| "Must be fit/athletic" (non-essential) | Discriminatory | Only include if truly essential with job justification |
| "Willing to relocate" (when not essential) | Can discriminate against those with caregiving obligations | Only require if truly necessary |

## CATEGORY 10: HARMFUL EXPECTATIONS

| Prohibited | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Available 24/7" | Unrealistic; exploitative | "On-call requirements [specific frequency]" |
| "No vacation first year" | Exploitative | Specify actual PTO policy |
| "Expected to work weekends" | Vague; exploitative | "Occasional weekend work may be required; compensated accordingly" |
| "Must be willing to do anything" | Vague; exploitative | List actual essential functions |
| "Superhero developer" | Unrealistic expectations | List specific skills and responsibilities |

## WHEN YOU'RE NOT SURE

**If unsure about a word or phrase:**
1. Ask yourself: "Would this exclude a protected class?"
2. Would someone in a protected class feel welcomed or discouraged?
3. Is there a more specific, neutral alternative?
4. If yes to #1 or #2, find an alternative
