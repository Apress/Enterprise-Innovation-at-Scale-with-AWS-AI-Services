## 2. Violations & Flags

### 🔴 VIOLATION 1 — On-Site Requirement May Conflict with ADA Reasonable Accommodation Obligations
**Severity:** HIGH
**Regulatory Basis:** Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), 42 U.S.C. § 12112

**Issue:** The posting states *"Remote work is not available for this role due to the sensitive nature of HR data"* — an absolute, unconditional prohibition on remote work. Under the ADA, remote work is a recognized form of reasonable accommodation. An employer cannot categorically exclude accommodations in a job posting without a documented, role-specific justification. Stating as a blanket policy that remote work is *never* available signals to candidates with disabilities that accommodations will be denied, which may chill applications from qualified candidates and constitute a constructive exclusion.

**The Distinction That Matters:** There is a meaningful legal difference between *"this role is designed as on-site"* (acceptable) and *"remote work is categorically unavailable under any circumstances"* (problematic). The ADA requires that each accommodation request be evaluated individually.

**Fix:** Replace the blanket prohibition with language that establishes the on-site preference while preserving the accommodation pathway:

> *"This role is designed as a full-time, on-site position in our San Francisco office. Reasonable accommodations, including schedule or location modifications, will be evaluated individually for qualified individuals with disabilities."*

---

### 🟡 FLAG 1 — "Positive Attitude" Language (About You Section)
**Severity:** MEDIUM
**Regulatory Basis:** EEOC guidance on neutral, job-related qualifications; potential ADEA proxy concern

**Issue:** The phrase *"You have a positive attitude"* appears in the "About You" section as a listed candidate attribute. While not facially illegal, vague personality descriptors like "positive attitude" are flagged in EEOC guidance as soft criteria that can be applied inconsistently and may disproportionately screen out candidates perceived as less conforming — including older workers who may be stereotyped as "set in their ways." These terms are also difficult to assess objectively, which creates downstream risk in adverse impact analysis.

**Fix:** Replace with a job-function-tied behavioral descriptor:

> *"Approaches employee interactions with professionalism and care, even in sensitive or challenging conversations."*

```
JOB POSTING - HR COORDINATOR
Supertron LLC - San Francisco, CA

Position: HR Coordinator
Location: San Francisco, CA (On-site)
Department: Human Resources
Reports To: HR Manager
Job Type: Full-Time

ABOUT THE ROLE:
Supertron LLC is seeking an HR Coordinator to support our HR team and assist
with day-to-day HR operations. You'll help manage employee records, coordinate
onboarding for new hires, process benefits, and support our HR team with
various administrative and operational tasks.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Maintain employee records and HR database (35% of time)
- Coordinate onboarding process for new hires (25% of time)
- Process benefits enrollment and changes (20% of time)
- Schedule meetings and manage calendars for HR team (10% of time)
- Support special projects as needed (10% of time)

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- 2+ years HR experience or equivalent administrative experience in an HR function
- Strong organizational and attention to detail
- Proficiency with HR systems and tools (HRIS, ATS, etc.)
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to handle confidential information appropriately
- High school diploma or equivalent; some college coursework or professional
  certification preferred

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- SHRM-CP or other HR certification
- Experience with benefits administration
- Proficiency with Google Workspace or Microsoft Office
- Customer service experience in HR or similar field

ABOUT YOU:
- You're detail-oriented and organized
- You're a strong communicator and enjoy supporting others
- You're reliable and can be trusted with sensitive information
- You're flexible and adapt to changing priorities
- You work collaboratively and contribute to a productive team environment
- You approach employee interactions with professionalism and care, even in
  sensitive or challenging conversations
- You understand the importance of confidentiality in HR

WORK LOCATION:
This role is designed as a full-time, on-site position in our San Francisco
office, with standard business hours of 9am–5pm. Reasonable accommodations,
including schedule or location modifications, will be evaluated individually
for qualified individuals with disabilities.

COMPENSATION:
Salary: $50,000 - $65,000 per year, based on experience

Benefits:
- Health insurance (medical, dental, vision)
- 401(k) with 3% employer match
- 15 days paid time off + 10 company holidays
- Wellness program
- Professional development support

WORK ENVIRONMENT:
This role involves working with employees at all levels and requires
discretion and professionalism. You'll work closely with the HR team and be
the first point of contact for HR-related employee questions. The position
may involve occasional emotionally challenging conversations related to
performance issues, terminations, or personal circumstances.

EQUAL OPPORTUNITY STATEMENT:
Supertron LLC is an Equal Opportunity Employer. We do not discriminate based
on race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, national origin, age,
disability, veteran status, genetic information, or other protected
characteristics. We are committed to providing reasonable accommodations for
qualified individuals with disabilities throughout the application, interview,
and employment process. If you require an accommodation, please contact HR at
careers@supertron.com.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Submit your resume, cover letter, and references to careers@supertron.com or
apply online at careers.supertron.com/hrcoordinator.

We will contact selected candidates to schedule interviews.
```
