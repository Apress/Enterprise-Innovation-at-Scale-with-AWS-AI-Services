## 2. Violations & Flags

### 🟡 LOW — "Growth Opportunity" Language (ADEA Proxy Risk)
**Location:** "About the Role" section
> *"This is a growth opportunity to develop your analytical and communication skills…"*

**Issue:** Phrases like "growth opportunity" and "develop your skills" can function as soft proxies for entry-level or younger candidates. While this language is not an explicit ADEA violation, it may inadvertently signal that the role is not appropriate for experienced or senior candidates. The Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA) prohibits discrimination against workers 40 and older, and coded language — even unintentional — can create exposure.

**Recommendation:** Reframe toward the role's impact rather than development framing. For example:
> *"This role offers meaningful analytical ownership with real impact on business decisions."*

---

### 🟡 LOW — "You'll" Framing in "About You" Section (Minor Tone Flag)
**Location:** "About You" section

**Issue:** The informal "You're..." framing is not a legal violation, but the section reads slightly like a personality checklist rather than a competency-based description. Personality-focused language (e.g., "You're curious," "You're organized") can occasionally draw scrutiny if it suggests subjective or culturally biased selection criteria. This is a LOW-confidence flag — it warrants a quick human review, not a mandatory revision.

**Recommendation:** Consider anchoring at least some bullets to observable behaviors or outcomes:
- "Curious about data" → "Applies data-driven inquiry to identify actionable insights"
- "Organized and manage multiple projects" → "Manages competing deadlines and multiple workstreams effectively"

## 3. Missing Elements

✅ **Pay Range** — Present and compliant. `$85,000 – $105,000/year` is disclosed with specific numbers as required by California SB 1162 (Labor Code § 244), effective January 1, 2024.

✅ **ADA Reasonable Accommodations Statement** — Present. The EEO section explicitly states accommodations are available and provides both an email and phone number for requests. This meets ADA requirements.

✅ **EEO Statement** — Present and inclusive, covering the full spectrum of protected classes.

✅ **Essential vs. Preferred Qualifications** — Clearly separated into "Required" and "Preferred" sections.

✅ **Location/Work Arrangement** — Clearly disclosed, including specific in-office days and flexibility language.

⚠️ **One Structural Best Practice Gap (RECOMMENDED, not required):**
The hybrid arrangement mentions "2 days/week in office" as the standard expectation, with flexibility noted. However, there is no explicit statement that remote/flexible arrangements may be available as a **reasonable accommodation** for disability — only as a general preference. While the general accommodation statement covers this implicitly, adding a brief note in the work arrangement section would reinforce clarity. This is a best practice recommendation, not a legal requirement.

## 5. Prioritized Recommendations

1. **(Low Priority)** Revise "growth opportunity" framing in the About the Role section to focus on impact and ownership rather than development — reduces soft ADEA proxy risk.
2. **(Low Priority)** Consider anchoring "About You" bullet points to observable competencies rather than personality traits.

4. **(Ongoing)** Confirm the posted salary range is reviewed quarterly against market data and updated if the range changes before the role is filled, per SB 1162 best practices.
