## 2. Violations & Flags

### 🟡 MEDIUM Severity

---

**Issue 1: Age Proxy Language — "Previous startup or high-growth company experience"**
*(Preferred Qualifications section)*

- **Why it's a problem:** Per company compliance documentation and ADEA guidance, "startup culture" and "high-growth company" are recognized as youth-coded proxy terms. They can imply a preference for younger workers who are stereotypically associated with startup environments and can subtly discourage older applicants.
- **Regulatory basis:** Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA); Supertron LLC Prohibited Language Reference Guide.
- **Fix:** Replace with language focused on specific, verifiable skills:
  > *"Experience working in dynamic, fast-paced product environments"* or *"Comfortable navigating ambiguity and rapid iteration cycles"*

*(Sources: Bu7U, Em2B, BsvW)*

---

**Issue 2: Pay Range — Multi-State Transparency Compliance**
*(Compensation section)*

- **What's there:** `$130,000 – $180,000 per year` — ✅ This satisfies California SB 1162, New York, Washington, and Colorado pay transparency requirements in format and specificity. A specific numerical range is provided, which is the core requirement.
- **Residual flag (LOW/MEDIUM):** The posting notes compensation varies by *"geographic location"* but provides only a single range. California SB 1162 and similar laws require that posted ranges be realistic and defensible. If the effective range meaningfully differs across geographies, best practice — and arguably California compliance — requires either region-specific ranges or explicit acknowledgment that the single range covers all locations.
- **Fix (Recommended):** If Supertron genuinely pays differently by region, consider adding a note such as:
  > *"The posted range reflects the full compensation band across all US locations. Actual compensation will vary based on location, skills, and experience."*
  > Or, if regional ranges differ significantly, list them explicitly.

*(Sources: W0z7, BzFc, xRmJ, 4tQo)*

---

### 🟢 LOW Severity / Informational

---

**Issue 3: Essential vs. Preferred Qualification Distinction — Adequate but Could Be Cleaner**

- **What's there:** The posting does distinguish "Required," "Preferred," and "Desired Attributes" — which is good. However, "Desired Attributes" (e.g., *"You're resilient"*, *"You're comfortable with ambiguity"*) are behavioral/personality traits that are not tied to observable, job-related functions.
- **Why it matters:** While not a direct legal violation, framing subjective personality attributes as formal evaluation criteria can introduce implicit bias and obscure the line between essential job functions and cultural preference — a concern under ADA essential-functions doctrine and EEOC guidance on job-related qualifications.
- **Fix:** Consider converting the "Desired Attributes" section into plain narrative (e.g., in the "About the Role" section) rather than a formal evaluation list, or clarify these are descriptions of the environment rather than formal requirements.

*(Sources: RPxw, dCPW, DfGU)*
